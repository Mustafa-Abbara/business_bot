EchoNest is a digital-memory studio. Our tagline is "Your memories, told your way." We turn a person's photos, voice recordings, written memories, important dates and captions into a personalized digital memory experience: a chronological story with captions, short narratives, key milestones, a visual timeline and a private shareable page. EchoNest was founded in 2023 by Maya Haddad, after the timeline she made for her grandfather's 80th birthday was passed around by the whole family. Since then we have delivered more than 300 memory projects. We are an online studio with a team based in Beirut, and we work with clients anywhere.

Our mission is to help people preserve meaningful moments by turning scattered digital memories into organized, personal stories that can be revisited and shared. A cloud-storage service answers "Where are my photos?" EchoNest answers "What story do these photos tell?" We serve families (family histories, anniversaries, birthdays, reunions), individuals (graduations, relationships, travels, personal milestones) and organizations (employee anniversaries, founder stories, company milestones).

We offer five services. A Memory Capsule ($79) is a compact digital story built around one event. An Event Story ($149) is designed for weddings, graduations, anniversaries and reunions. A Life Timeline ($299) combines memories from many years into one chronological story. The Legacy Edition ($499) is our premium service for preserving a person's life story from interviews, photographs, documents and memories. A Corporate Story is a company-history package for startups and organizations; it has no fixed price and is quoted after a consultation.

Our team: Maya Haddad, Founder & Creative Director, specializes in digital storytelling and archival design. Karim Saad, Memory Experience Designer, creates the timelines, layouts and visual stories. Lina Farhat, Client Experience Manager, handles customer communication and project coordination.

Policies. Typical projects take 5 to 10 business days. Every project includes two revision rounds. Customer material is treated as private project material. Customers may cancel before production begins. We support photos, written memories, captions and standard audio recordings. We do not currently offer film restoration, physical photo restoration, professional videography, or forensic recovery of deleted files. Our team works Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM (Beirut time). To start a project, leave your name and email and Lina Farhat's team will reply within one business day.
