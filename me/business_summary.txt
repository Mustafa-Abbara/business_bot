Cedar Code Academy is a coding and robotics tutoring centre in Hamra, Beirut. Our mission is to make practical programming and STEM skills accessible to students aged 12 to 25 through small-group, project-based learning. We were founded in 2021, when our three founders started a free weekend study group for engineering students in their shared apartment. Since then we have taught more than 400 students.

We offer five services. Python Foundations is an 8-week group course (two 90-minute sessions per week) for complete beginners, priced at $240 for the full course. University CS Tutoring is one-on-one help with university courses such as data structures, algorithms and programming labs, at $25 per hour. The Web Development Bootcamp is a 12-week course covering HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React, priced at $450. The Robotics & Arduino Club is a Saturday club for ages 12 to 16, at $60 per month. We also run Corporate & School Workshops, which are custom trainings for companies and schools; these do not have a fixed price and are quoted after a consultation with the client.

Our team is led by three co-founders. Rami Haddad, Director, is a former full-stack software engineer and teaches the Web Development Bootcamp. Lina Khoury, Head of Curriculum, holds an MSc in Computer Engineering and leads Python Foundations and University CS Tutoring. Omar Saade, Robotics Lead, is an electrical engineer who runs the Robotics & Arduino Club.

What makes us different: groups never have more than 6 students, every course ends with a real project that students can show in a portfolio, classes are taught in English with explanations in Arabic whenever needed, and the first session of any group course is a free trial. Python Foundations and University CS Tutoring are also available online.

We are open Monday to Friday from 2:00 PM to 9:00 PM and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM; we are closed on Sundays. To book a course or request a workshop quote, leave your name and email and our team will reply within one business day.
